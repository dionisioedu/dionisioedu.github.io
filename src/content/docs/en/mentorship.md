## Who it's for

You code, you study, you apply for roles — and still feel like you're spinning in place. It isn't a lack of effort. It's a lack of direction.

- **Junior dev or student (18–24):** studying a lot, unsure if you're on the right path, still no first role.
- **Mid-level dev (25–32):** doing the same thing for three years, wanting senior, remote or international — and unsure which jump matters.
- **Senior dev (32–40):** wanting to move into high performance, critical systems, data or technical leadership — and needing criteria to decide.

## Who it's NOT for

Straight talk, because it saves us both time:

- People who want me to do the work. Mentorship isn't private tutoring or code delivery.
- People who want "just a motivational talk." You leave here with tasks, deadlines and a written plan.
- People who won't execute between sessions. Without execution there's no useful diagnosis.
- People looking for therapy or emotional support. That isn't what I offer — the focus is always technical and career.

## How it works

An arc with a beginning, a middle and an end. Four sessions, not loose calls.

### Session 1 — Diagnosis (90 min)

We map where you actually are: stack, projects, portfolio, study routine, application history and real goal (not the one that looks good on LinkedIn).

**You leave with:** a Diagnosis Document — strengths, concrete gaps, and your next 90 days prioritized.

### Session 2 — Technical plan (60 min)

We design the roadmap for your specific target: what to study, in what order, and — just as important — what to *stop* studying. Plus what to build to actually prove competence.

**You leave with:** a 90-day roadmap + one or two portfolio projects that tell the right story.

### Session 3 — Execution and feedback (60 min)

You bring what you built. We review code, decisions, the project — and correct the route where you got stuck.

**You leave with:** concrete corrections and your next block of tasks.

### Session 4 — Market strategy (60 min)

Positioning, resume and LinkedIn, technical interview prep, salary negotiation — or the specialization decision, if that's the case.

**You leave with:** a positioned-resume checklist + an application plan for the coming weeks.

Between sessions: **a weekly written check-in** on an agreed channel. Execution questions, not a parallel course.

## FAQ

### Is this therapy or coaching?

No. It's technical and career mentorship. The focus is always stack, project, positioning and professional decisions. I don't work with emotional or clinical issues.

### Does it work for any language?

The core — career, portfolio, positioning, study, market — applies to any stack. I have real depth in **C++, high-performance systems, data and software engineering**, and that's where I can help at the most technical level.

### Do I need to know C++?

No. Most mentees are developers from other stacks who want career direction. C++ comes in when it's the target.

### How much time per week do I need?

Between 5 and 10 hours of execution per week make the 90 days productive. Less than that, and the plan stretches.

### What if I don't like it?

The Diagnosis Session is the entry point precisely for that: low commitment, immediate result. If afterwards you don't want to continue, you don't — and you leave with the plan in hand.

### How does payment work?

Upfront or in installments, with a 7-day guarantee. Rescheduling up to 24h in advance.
